# ProcureLink — additional requirements (Agent 1, research)

This compares the planned ProcureLink scope with standard source-to-pay (S2P) and procure-to-pay (P2P) capabilities and with the Vietnamese rules a mid-size private buyer has to follow. The largest gap is **invoice-to-pay**. Today the flow stops at GRN, so there is no e-invoice capture, no 3-way match and no VAT handling. Two **controls** are also missing: sealed bids and an approvals inbox with delegation, plus quality rejection at receipt. Most other items extend existing screens. Only 2 NEW screens are proposed.
Status is judged against the scope list in the task (Login … Admin console). Every requirement states how it hangs off **Material ID**.

Key facts used:
- **E-invoices.** The ký hiệu has a C/K prefix, the year, then a type letter, then 2 seller characters (e.g. `1C26TMP`). Validity is checked on hoadondientu.gdt.gov.vn ("Đã được cấp mã hóa đơn"). Since 01/06/2025, an adjustment or replacement invoice needs a written agreement, and the buyer declares tax when it receives a compliant invoice.
- **VAT.** The rate is cut from 10% to **8% from 01/07/2025 to 31/12/2026**. **Metal products** and mining products (except coal) are among the excluded groups.
- **MST format.** Enterprise MST = 10 digits; branch or dependent unit = 10+3 digits. Individuals and household businesses use the 12-digit personal ID number from 01/07/2025.
- **Bid security.** 1–1,5% of the package price for works/mixed ≤ 20 tỷ and for goods/non-consulting services ≤ 10 tỷ; otherwise 1,5–3%. Returned ≤ 14 days after the result is approved.
- **Performance security.** 2–10% of the contract price.

## Out of scope (deliberately left out)
- **Payment execution, bank files, GL postings, tax declarations.** These stay in the accounting / ERP system. ProcureLink stops at "Ready for payment" and exports (R-02, R-20).
- **Issuing e-invoices.** The company is the buyer here. Issuing is done in its own e-invoice provider's software.
- **Full public-procurement compliance** (national e-procurement system muasamcong, mandatory forms, published plans). The company is private and only borrows practices: sealed bids, bid/performance security, two envelopes, COI.
- **Catalogues / punch-out / guided buying marketplace.** A mid-size buyer with a controlled Material ID master does not need them. The Material ID lookup in Create PR covers it.
- **OCR / AI invoice extraction.** Vietnamese e-invoices arrive as structured XML, so parsing is enough.
- **Inventory / warehouse management, MRP / demand planning.** GRN is the only stock touch-point.
- **E-contract signing platform / CA integration.** Only the signature *status* is shown (R-21). Signing happens in an external tool.
- **Multi-entity / multi-company accounting, supply-chain finance, dynamic discounting, ESG / carbon scoring, native mobile apps.** These are beyond a mid-size internal app. The responsive web pages already cover approvals and receipts on a phone.
